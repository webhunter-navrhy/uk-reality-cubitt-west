Situated in a quiet cul de sac location this good-sized detached house offers versatile living space, boasting a large kitchen/diner with double doors out to the rear garden. A work from home office is conveniently located on the ground floor and the large lounge benefits from a wood burning stove. Upstairs offers great sized bedrooms and a family bathroom. There is off-road parking for several vehicles and the rear garden has a summer house and several areas for eating and entertaining. This could be the perfect family home with primary and secondary schools within 1 mile of the property.
